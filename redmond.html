Permits

How permitting works in Redmond

We pull the permits and schedule the inspections. You do not deal with the city.

Permits for Redmond go through City of Redmond Planning & Community Development.

  • Redmond uses the shared Eastside MyBuildingPermit.com portal.
  • Interior non-structural remodels are the fastest path; cutting an engineered truss is not.
  • Four to six weeks of review is normal for structural residential work.

Timelines change. We confirm the current review times when we submit, and the date we submitted is in your project schedule.

Questions

Redmond homeowners ask us

Can I open up the ceiling in my 1990s house?

Sometimes, but homes of that era typically use engineered trusses, and you cannot cut one without an engineered repair detail. We check the attic before promising anything.

What does a properly sound-isolated home office cost?

It depends on whether we are working inside an existing room or building walls. The ballpark difference between «a room with a door» and «a room you can take calls in» is smaller than most people expect — we will price both at the walkthrough.

Do you work around our schedule if we work from home?

Yes. Most Redmond clients are in the house during the build, so we agree on quiet hours and noisy-work windows before we start.
